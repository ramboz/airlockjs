## Slice 060-07 - Match approved forms automatically

**Goal:** An adopter enables automatic matching for approved forms and obtains
native-equivalent identity collection without giving a chamber ambient DOM access.

**DoR:**
- [ ] 060-06 establishes explicit identity semantics and all inherited policy/
      routing/reference prerequisites.
- [ ] Owner supplies feature eligibility/settings, native configuration
      provenance and safe synthetic form/identity fixtures through private handles.
      Unreadable public help or state keys are not proof of the active feature.
- [ ] Parent reviews exact bounded field/root/event capture and measured
      work/retention limits against source seams and stable-core contracts.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. On an owned synthetic form, automatic matching off/on with manual matching
   absent/present produces the native collection triggers and complete
   measurement sequence through the actual chamber and mediated dispatch.
   The host only captures registered permitted fields/events; the chamber
   determines mapping, normalization and hashing.
2. Field changes before/after events, dynamic forms and anonymous/account
   changes preserve native timing, precedence and same-input digest values.
   Cover each eligible discovered identity field and account-specific setting;
   a private `aud`-like field name alone is not automatic-feature support.
3. Sensitive/unrelated/unregistered fields, revoked consent, stale snapshots and
   unauthorized root references cannot be captured or sent contrary to policy.
   No selector evaluation, raw DOM node, full mutation stream or arbitrary
   executable callback crosses the capability boundary.
4. Native configuration/rule updates and missing/blocked settings are
   provenance-qualified per Pixel. New/private encoding or collection branches
   become explicit blockers, not manual-only substitutes or discarded fields.
5. No raw identity egress or main-thread hashing occurs. Authorized same-input
   values are compared privately before redaction; public operator reports show
   source class, policy version and difference category, not identity values.
6. Alter a permitted input, omit a required field, leak an unrelated field,
   change a digest or reuse stale identity. Each corresponding positive/
   forbidden-collection control fails under C1-C9; equal redaction tokens fail
   the value-proof requirement.
7. Record DOM observation cost, snapshot volume and dispatch latency against
   pinned measured conditions. Missing limits block readiness; no speculative
   fixed CWV band or blanket DOM grant is introduced to make the feature fit.

**DoD:**
- [ ] Synthetic form/identity, configuration, denial and mutation matrices pass.
- [ ] Targeted real browser/chamber/capability/contract regressions and lint pass.
- [ ] Independent compliance, craft, architecture and reconciliation reviews pass.
- [ ] The adopter procedure exposes eligible fields, settings provenance and gaps.

**Anti-horizontal-phasing check:** The adopter completes automatic matching on
an actual controlled form and sees the resulting governed native-equivalent wire.

## Assumptions

- Unverified: native eligible-field discovery and timing can be reproduced
  through finite capture-only operations without broad form observation.
- Unverified: required feature settings and native encoding semantics are
  accessible and measured cost fits the approved budgets; current state keys
  establish neither.
