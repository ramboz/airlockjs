## Slice 060-11 - Diagnose connected product collection

**Goal:** An operator can verify the replacement's collection in Meta tooling
and connected catalog/audience workflows, and distinguish wire correctness from
matching or attribution outcomes.

**DoR:**
- [ ] Automatic/lifecycle/interoperability journeys 08/09/10 and their inherited
      event/identity/policy prerequisites are available.
- [ ] Owner supplies dedicated Events Manager access, catalog/feed and safe
      rule fixtures where required, plus separately approved lawful matching/
      reporting/attribution observations. No universal campaign/spend prerequisite.
- [ ] Native diagnostic/version pins and feature-specific measurement windows,
      sample requirements, uncertainty treatment and acceptance criteria are fixed
      before the experiment; no matching benchmark is invented from synthetic users.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. An operator follows the adopter procedure from actual chamber event through
   official dispatch/response to a correlated owned Test Events/Events Manager
   receipt, with missing/delayed/aggregate evidence explicitly classified.
   The historical owner-confirmed normal-browser receipt remains attributed,
   not retroactively paired or called agent-witnessed.
2. Using a separately approved isolated pinned helper/Meta Ads Data Advisor
   profile, event detections, fields, warnings and native-absent replacement
   statuses are compared to native behavior and actual requests. Read-only
   diagnostics do not authorize its privileged setup/fix automation; no fake
   successful badge/internal hook masks a detection gap.
3. ViewContent/AddToCart/Purchase and other applicable catalog collection
   preserve exact product/product-group IDs, quantities and money against the
   owned feed. Observe actual catalog source recognition/mismatch diagnostics;
   differing public requirement wording remains evidence-backed, not a silent
   restriction to one accepted property shape.
4. Owned custom conversion and website-audience rules receive native-equivalent
   event/URL/property inputs. Policy rejection, unavailable rule fields and
   delayed recognition are visible. Collection is distinct from backend
   audience membership/modeling; no new campaign/backend engine is implemented.
5. Lawful matched inputs and approved native/replacement outcome observations
   compare matching quality, reporting and attribution under the predeclared
   criteria/windows, with correlation and uncertainty limits. Synthetic hashes,
   request 200, event receipt and deduplication are not matching/attribution proof.
   Missing access/traffic/outcomes blocks that claim, not a waived obligation.
6. Wrong catalog ID, removed event/property, changed normalized identity,
   unpaired/missing receipt, diagnostic false success and absent attribution
   controls fail or become inconclusive under C1-C9. Operator summary states
   per-layer results rather than one green receipt standing for all layers.
7. Public reports expose safe classifications/counts only. Private original
   request/receipt relationships stay authorized and separate. Initial collection
   does not require campaign launch/billing; any feature/outcome that truly needs
   more resources has an explicit later finite owner grant, not implicit spend.

**DoD:**
- [ ] Actual paired collection, catalog/rule and approved outcome controls meet
      their predeclared criteria; unavailable criteria cannot close full support.
- [ ] Targeted diagnostics/browser/chamber/report-contract regressions and lint pass.
- [ ] Independent compliance, craft, architecture and reconciliation reviews pass.
- [ ] Operator instructions and claim-specific evidence/access limits are accurate.

**Anti-horizontal-phasing check:** The operator completes real connected-product
verification with actionable differences, not a UI-only diagnostic panel.
