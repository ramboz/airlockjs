# Proposed report-fidelity rubric

Assess only whether the candidate report faithfully describes the supplied, hypothetical evidence. 1.0: it identifies observed and unavailable layers and avoids unsupported delivery, receipt or correlation claims. 0.0: it asserts a higher evidence layer than supplied, or conceals a material uncertainty. Insufficient source evidence must be explicitly reported as inconclusive. This rubric cannot establish private wire correctness or product truth.

No human rating or approval is implied. Transport is proposed native Copilot with gpt-6.1-sol; runtime, sample parameters, threshold and native decoding consent remain unresolved. No scoring is enabled.
