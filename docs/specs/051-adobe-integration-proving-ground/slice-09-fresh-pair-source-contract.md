### Proposed selection, restoration and stop procedure

This procedure describes future work only. It grants no mutation or entry.
Before a future attempt, the owner must separately accept the admission
decision and exact live authority, complete a current preflight and prove
independent native stop and operator presence. The operator must capture an
authoritative, current baseline for the exact owned activity, environment,
workspace, schedule, enabled state, targeting and complete experience split.
The operator must establish that both selection and the exact inverse are
representable and permitted by the approved surface. If any baseline,
permission, selection or inverse is uncertain, stop before changes.

A possible selection is a temporary 100/0 split for one explicitly identified
experience, applied to the same fixed activity for both arms. This is a
proposal, not a current authorization or proof that the surface can do it.
Verify the saved selection and routing before either arm; preserve each arm's
original URL, event data and private correlation. Do not infer the selected
experience from the existing 50/50 split. After the pair or any interruption,
the designated owner restores the entire captured baseline and verifies the
saved values and routing. If the current state no longer matches the state
installed by the operator, stop and escalate rather than overwrite another
change. A failed or unverified inverse blocks future admission.

The separately designated owner must have an independently invocable stop
action for the exact approved activity and native process targets, identified
in the future live package before any effect. Stop may only quiesce those
targets and begin the approved restoration; it must not start, resume or retry
an arm. Current `stopFreshPair` returns a blocked result and performs no
action. Source simulations do not prove that the owner can invoke stop after
caller loss, that filesystem races are controlled, or that native processes
have ended. Those remain explicit pre-admission gates.
