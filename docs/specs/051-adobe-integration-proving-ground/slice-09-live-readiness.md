**Disposition: blocked for live execution.** The offline comparison ran the
unchanged official Adobe Web SDK in stock and governed chamber arms. It used
synthetic responses and a local sink. It does not establish a live Adobe
journey or deployed Edge Delivery Services (EDS) adoption.

This assessment is based on documentation checkpoint
`4ad00bcde5f36fd59cbaef058147093296aa3f10`. The executed offline source was
`7e8e100f472f24e6e39c733c13ea2cd18a72fc38`. The owner approved work needed

not a fresh exact live grant, renewed old budget, or a numeric AI-credit cap.
Native exact-cap approval was unavailable.

## What is supported

One coherent offline roster passed all 17 arms. It used the real stock
integration, actual worker and governed host, and pinned official SDK. It
recorded 46 SDK-generated local receipts and held two controller-generated
endpoint and tenant fence attempts, for 48 attempts total. Positive arms
rendered a synthetic offer and observed a trusted click. No-consent, no-offer,
non-render, rejected and malformed response, and untrusted-interaction cases
were exercised within that offline fixture. The payload case stripped its
denied field and dispatched the remaining body to the local sink. Endpoint and
tenant fence stimuli were controller-generated. These results have scoped
independent reviews; they are not live control results.

Epoch 2 is closed: 24/24 native invocations, 15/15 rosters, 98/136 contexts,
83/84 realm probes and 828/828 dispatch reservations were charged. Its
source/native stop was `2026-11-03T18:32:37Z`; its record-only stop was
`2026-11-03T19:02:37Z`. Both have expired. The original eight active hours
are spent. There is no refund, new root or new consumption namespace.

## What blocks a live pair

- **No admitted route.** Public `run` returns `source-unqualified` before
  effects. Historical retry returns `historical-retry-unqualified`. The
  operator pins a different historical worktree and reads retained originals;
  its prepared job and source pins cannot be treated as a fresh package. Its
  consumed-job stop requires an actual matching consumed record. It is not a
  qualified stop for a new run. Caller-loss, lifetime, real filesystem race
  and consumed-stop safety remain unqualified. The offline network-denied
  helper is not a live runner. No flag, fixture, root transplant or
  manufactured original may restore admission.
- **No current handoff or setup.** The coordinator reported both
  `AIRLOCK_ADOBE_PRIVATE_INPUTS_DIR` and `ADOBE_CREDENTIAL_FILE` unset; they
  were also unset in this writer's environment. Do not search old private
  files. Target cleanup remains UNKNOWN. Historical identifiers, dates and
  grants do not establish current ownership, setup or quiet.
- **No approved exact live package.** A fresh owned domain, environment and
  object manifest, current permissions, all 12 required preparation checks,
  operator-ready observation, served and worker byte pins, exclusive
  coordination, and reviewed stop and cleanup allocation are not established. The
  plan must account for prior charges and UNKNOWNs before any effects.
  Finite contexts, requests, bytes, administrative calls, cleanup calls,
  observation windows and any additional costs still need exact approval.
  ADR-0045's historical two contexts and five requests per arm are not fresh
  available allowance and do not cover the separate required negative controls.

Old C remains FAIL. The R1 supplied-record checker retains false actual and
provenance claims. Live new-run and historical retry admission remain
withdrawn, and 051-09 and 051 remain IN_PROGRESS. ADR-0045 selects only a
conditional human-supervised approach. Proposed ADR-0059 and ADR-0060 are not

## Proposed next operational scope

First resolve the admitted-route and independent-stop blocker under a separately
reviewed, exact scope and fresh owner authorization. Establish whether the
existing bounded seams can safely support it without relaxing the quarantine,
inventing a new consumption namespace or adding a generic service. If not,
stop and report the specific gap. Do not launch a partial live adapter.
Independently resolve the historical cleanup UNKNOWN and cumulative accounting
to the extent needed for safe admission; unresolved conflicts stop the pair.

Before any effect, bind an exact private manifest and permissions, unchanged
setup, all 12 current required preflight checks, operator presence and
independent stop access, precise traffic and mutation scope, inverse and
readback, and finite costs. Review the exact package and obtain fresh explicit
approval. Bind the same decoded Alloy 2.31.1 bytes in both arms and verify
deployed site and worker pins. Its pinned SHA-256 is
`7dd09409bb07d47b1b2289eec4ca15d67084a85a3d832bef6c73180889da32e8`.
The reference Adobe Client Data Layer (ACDL) 3.0.1 bytes differ from npm by
the final line feed; do not trim, repin or claim byte identity.

After admission, run one stock arm and then one chamber arm in separate fresh
contexts, closing the first before starting the second. For each, observe
native consent, proposition fetch, safe visible render, a page event with
native DISPLAY, a distinct custom event, and native INTERACT after a trusted
click on the intended owned control. Capture original returned results and
errors. Stop on divergence, lost supervision, unexpected traffic or uncertain
cleanup. Preserve partial evidence and UNKNOWNs rather than retrying.

Freeze a closed comparison inventory before traffic: destination and action,
method, tenant, consent, request order and count, page/custom payloads, response
and caller result, offer content, and original proposition id, scope and
scopeDetails in each notification. Join original request bytes, governed
admissions, body witnesses, responses, SDK results, host render and trusted
input by request identity and time. Bind ordinal, unique request UUID, URL
hash, method, content type, original body hash and byte count across each
request, admission and body witness. Join the fetch response to the returned
proposition and applied content. Retain private raw captures.

The offline rules are candidates for fresh live review, not automatic live
normalization: URL `requestId` must be a unique UUID joined within each arm;
`/events/<index>/xdm/timestamp` must fit the observed window and order;
`/events/<index>/xdm/identityMap/ECID/<index>/id` must keep its role and
within-arm continuity; and `/meta/queueTimeMillis` must be a bounded observed
duration on corresponding event requests only. Require symmetric presence
where applicable, keep raw values, and review each rule against live captures.
Unknown or asymmetric fields block comparison. Never strip identity, routing,
event type, ordering or proposition references wholesale.

Plan and approve **separate finite negative controls** for no offer,
non-render, denied consent, endpoint, tenant, payload, error, identity,
revocation, duplicate, retry and lifetime behavior. The offline cases above
are local evidence only. Identity, revocation, duplicate, retry and lifetime
are not qualified by that roster. A control that cannot reach its intended
seam is unexercised, not passed. Define observation windows and expected
effects before any control traffic.

Actual wire, return, render and native notifications remain mandatory.
Downstream reporting counters are a nonblocking follow-up under ADR-0039.
After admitted evidence, the next bounded adoption slice should cover
EDS/aem-martech installation, phase wiring and rollback. It does not complete
the wider compatibility portfolio, performance gate or release.
