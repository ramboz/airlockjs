## Decision Options Considered

### Option A: Keep all native work blocked

- **Pros:** Adds no native execution or ownership surface.
- **Cons:** Leaves the independent stop path unqualified for the later pair.

### Option B: Two fixed supervised blank controls

- **Pros:** Exercises the specific foreground ownership and stop path without
  admitting SDK or Adobe work.
- **Cons:** Requires an available owner, a trusted coordinator and a
  separately reviewed admission boundary. Two attempts do not establish
  universal process or filesystem safety. The owner selected the
  operator-attested direction for source work, not physical allocation or
  acceptance of this decision.

### Option C: Independent issuer authentication

- **Pros:** Could authenticate approval independently of a same-user
  coordinator if backed by a real external trust anchor.
- **Cons:** No independent issuer, key or receipt service has been handed
  off. This option is blocked, not replaced by a same-user key or broker.

Reusing the historical campaign or building a generic service remains
outside scope. Historical operation has different guards and unavailable
originals.

## Recommended Decision

Propose Option B with the following owner-selected operator-attested trust
boundary, subject to source review and eventual acceptance of this exact
decision. The real human operator and privileged same-user coordinator
are trusted roles. The owner personally runs the reviewed future commands
in an external native terminal and confirms readiness for the exact job.
The coordinator retains an auditable record of the owner's acceptance and
confirmation without claiming to have directly observed the terminal or
independently authenticated human presence.
Software validates closed scope, source, owner and job bindings, receipt
consistency, reservation and expiry. It does not cryptographically prove
human identity or prevent a malicious same-user coordinator from forging
records or bypassing its own workflow. File ownership, a boolean, a nonce,
job data or model-piped input alone is not owner readiness. Missing actual
human action or owner confirmation blocks the case. This source-only
window provides neither.

For the first controller-initiated filesystem operation, propose fixed
initial timestamps supplied with the reviewed command by the trusted
operator. START checks its initial expiry and hard stop; STOP checks the
hard stop without reapplying START expiry. The immutable job and future
physical plan must then bind those inputs exactly, with no later widening.
They are consistency bounds, not owner authentication or authorization.
The contract defines the fixed bootstrap path, limits and descriptor
handling. This does not claim to bound Node's startup or module loading,
garbage collection or OS completion.

After durable same-case consumption and the original binding and attestation
checks, propose a stop-only phase for the live START producer. After START
expiry, that phase uses the originally supplied hard stop and the original
wall and monotonic tracking for fixed same-case STOP reads, coordination
and retained-handle closure. The original 45+10 qualification interval
does not restart. An unfinished qualification becomes permanently UNKNOWN
and stays charged when that interval ends; later safety work cannot upgrade
it or enable the second case. The exact accepted decision and separately
approved future physical plan must expressly bind the distinct safety-only
scope before it can be used. The contract lists its permitted operations and
disjoint safety records. It admits no new START work, ordinary publication,
retry or new process. This proposed clarification needs a new frame review
and does not grant physical authority.

The proposed source boundary separates the pinned worktree and installed
Playwright package bytes from the selected browser-owned bundle. A fixed
manifest binds the Chromium headless-shell executable and every file and
internal link in its selected Playwright installation, including helper and
framework files. The reviewed command derives the one allowed macOS
Playwright cache location from the current owner's account home, the pinned
Playwright revision and architecture; a caller does not select a root.
The manifest and job are bound to each other and to the future physical
allocation. Missing, changed or unprovable bundle identity blocks START.
The contract defines the proposed manifest and validation rules.

The OS kernel, system libraries and installed Node runtime are an explicitly
trusted platform baseline, not browser bundle files. The future job records
the platform and Node baseline and validation checks consistency. This does
not hash or independently qualify that baseline. The trusted owner and
same-user coordinator remain capable of bypassing or forging the workflow.
The current source refusal remains in place until the amended frame and
source proof gates clear; neither the manifest design nor local package
hashes are evidence of an actual installed browser's provenance.

Propose, for a separately allocated future operation and subject to explicit
acceptance of the exact reviewed decision, a distinct `native-control`
purpose for only the two fixed blank cases in the contract. It has a private immutable job, expiration, source and
decision digest, owner binding and pre-effect reservation. A source
inspection, flag, fabricated approval or remapped historical root cannot
become that job or an SDK job. Exclusive intent and case reservation
precede acquisition; every attempted or UNKNOWN case stays charged. No
retry or replay is allowed. The concrete proposed attestation and stop
protocol is in the contract.

New START admission and launch require the entire 45-second case plus the
ten-second observation allowance to fit before the earlier of the job's
admission expiry and the approved hard stop. Check that fit before
reservation and again before launch. New work remains refused at or after
admission expiry.

An owner/job-bound STOP for that same already-consumed case remains available
after admission expiry and strictly before the approved hard stop. It may
only revoke admission and request closure by the live producer of that
case's verified owned handle. It must verify the original owner, immutable
job, accepted decision and source bindings, but must not reject solely
because the original admission or operator-readiness confirmation has aged
out. It grants no start, resume, retry or allocation extension. No new stop
effect starts at or after the hard stop; an incomplete or unprovable closure
is UNKNOWN. A separately approved, finite cleanup interval may allow only
closing file or directory descriptors already owned by that process. It
permits no new publication, lock removal or browser/SDK effect. The contract
defines its binding and bounds; no such future interval is granted here.

Allow a fixed owner-supervised foreground command and a separately invocable
stop-only command. The producer retains the actual browser-server handle
returned by its launch. The stop command records job-bound revocation; the
live producer consumes it and closes that retained handle. No caller chooses
an endpoint from a file. The first command must hold the actual owned handle
before reporting ready. If stop arrives during the real unresolved launch,
a subsequently acquired handle is closed inside the hard stop without
creating a context or admitting SDK work. Delayed publication or a mock is
not proof that launch was unresolved. Acquisition that settles too late,
producer loss, or an absent, unprovable or timed-out handle is UNKNOWN, not
a successful stop. A recorded request alone is not closure. No stale PID
or name-based process kill is authority. The stop command cannot start,
resume or retry an operation.

The decision permits no Adobe mutation, deployment, external page navigation,
SDK acquisition, evaluation or dispatch, and no use of owner login profiles
or credential-bearing browser environment. The blank page has default-deny
network controls. Unexpected observed egress stops qualification. Page/CDP
observations do not establish host-wide network isolation.

The old public run/retry, historical roots, consumed reservations and original
readers remain unchanged. No widening of core, default grants, endpoint,
tenant, cookie or frozen APIs is permitted. A general runner, second
sandbox, broker, daemon or arbitrary command/callback surface is excluded.

Live SDK entry remains separately blocked. A future live decision and exact
owner allocation, current preparation/preflight and source/served pins,
deterministic owned experience and inverse, actual operator readiness, and
independently available native stop are required. This decision cannot
upgrade blank controls to live SDK authority.

## Consequences

**Becomes easier:**
- The owner can test a narrow start/stop procedure without Adobe traffic.

**Becomes harder:**
- Native ownership, independent stop and unknown outcomes need explicit
  accounting and an external operator. The two controls may not complete.
- The result does not qualify caller-loss handling, recursive descendant
  closure, filesystem-race safety, SDK behavior or full 051-09.

## Assumptions

- A live foreground producer can retain the actual Playwright server handle
  and consume a job-bound stop request without selecting a stored endpoint.
  Existing source shows acquisition and handle publication at
  `probes/adobe-compatibility/supervised-journey-operator.mjs:978-987`;
  it does not demonstrate this protocol. Producer-loss cleanup is unqualified.
- A stop can be observed while the real launch promise remains unresolved.
  If launch settles too quickly, the second case is not demonstrated and
  cannot be retried. A delayed synthetic observation is not native proof.
- The selected local browser and installed Playwright can enforce the stated
  blank-page controls without downloading packages or browser binaries.
- The fixed manifest can account for the selected installed bundle, including
  internal links and helpers, without arbitrary root mapping or an unsafe
  race. Its actual layout, compatibility and provenance are not established
  by this source-only proposal.
- Already-owned file and directory descriptors can be closed within an
  independently approved cleanup bound without new publication. Runtime
  garbage collection and OS completion remain unqualified.
- Owner presence in a separate native terminal is available and the human
  personally acts and confirms readiness for each future physical case.
  The coordinator does not claim direct observation or independent identity
  verification. This document, a receipt or a model-piped ready line alone
  does not establish that actual human action.

## Kill criteria

- A safe exclusive reservation, exact owned handle, independent stop or
  late-handle cleanup needs a general platform or a protected-boundary change.
- A required gate, owner presence, installed browser, time bound or proof of
  ownership is missing; a conflict, unexpected egress or UNKNOWN occurs.
- A case requires credentials, Adobe access, an SDK, a replay or a historical
  allocator to make progress.

## Open questions

Can the real acquisition window be reached by an external stop without
inventing a paused or synthetic acquisition? Can the retained-handle
protocol establish closure while the producer remains live, and does it
meet the intended independent-stop scope? Both remain unqualified until
frame and source review and the bounded controls. A negative answer stops
the checkpoint rather than widening its architecture.
