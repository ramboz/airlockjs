# ADR-0053: One additional lineage-bound Google reference

The original Google allowance is spent. This proposal sets a possible contract
for one additional inspected stock reference. It keeps the original slots
immutable and requires new source qualification. The tradeoff is more authority
and review machinery for a reference that still cannot prove browser TLS parity.

## Status

Proposed (2026-11-10). This number is a local provisional reservation and
requires collision checking before integration.

The owner selected **source-only proposal preparation**. This record has not
passed its independent ADR frame critique or received owner acceptance. It
allocates no authority, case, attempt, slot, control run or execution window.
It authorizes no source implementation, browser, SDK or vendor work.

## Context

The native Google entry is closed and the original allowance is spent.

The [admission consumer](../../rig/inspected-google-admission.mjs#L109-L149)
still requires the fixed original authority and unspent original stock slot.
Its [native entry](../../rig/inspected-google-admission.mjs#L310-L326) always
refuses execution. The existing additional-authority
[reservation](../../rig/native-stock-capture.mjs#L126-L174) and
[native lease/collector](../../rig/inspected-transport.mjs#L162-L174) are
Meta-specific. The shown Google transport is
[literal loopback](../../rig/inspected-transport.mjs#L189-L223). Removing
the closed-entry error would not produce a qualified native Google transport.

The [native cohort selector](../../rig/inspected-google-cohort.mjs#L26-L41)
still requires Chrome 156.0.8078.80. Its
[qualification consumer](../../rig/inspected-google-admission.mjs#L146-L290)
requires 84 exact H1/H2 controls, source/cohort pins, retained originals,
independent child completion and bound review documents. The inherited Chrome
157 SDK-free run covers seven local cases, not those 84 controls. It is
historical evidence, not current admission or this writer's browser observation.

That run's raw overall result is INCONCLUSIVE, full compatibility is false and
interruption-loss qualification is false. All four current157 local grants are
consumed. Helpers remain UNKNOWN/manual. Complete descendants, agent-loss
cleanup, opaque HTTP and the hidden browser parent/302 remain UNKNOWN. Earlier
failures remain FAILED. The prohibited historical main dependency read/copy and
out-of-scope controlled Meta-fake deviations remain. Clean custody and earlier
or dynamic-write absence remain NOT_ATTESTED. Earlier retrospective,
transcript-only and tool-output provenance limits are not repaired here.

## Decision Options Considered

1. **Keep the native reference blocked.** This avoids a new authority contract
   but leaves the native evidence gap open.
2. **Reuse or replace the original authority or slot namespace.** Reject this.
   Failure or a spent case cannot be refunded or retried through a renamed path.
3. **Create one separately reviewed additional Google stock lineage.**
   Recommend this option. It preserves the original authority and slots and
   retains the native safety conditions below. It requires new current-source
   qualification and explicit future admission.

## Recommended Decision

If accepted, select option 3 prospectively for exactly one additional
Google-only inspected stock reference. This is a narrow exception to the
original-slot-only requirement in ADR-0045, ADR-0047 and ADR-0048 for the
new lineage described here. It does not amend those Accepted records, reopen
their original slots, or waive their other native safety conditions. No
chamber authority follows.

### Authority and consumer

A later, separately issued owner-authorized grant may allocate one additional
reference under its own authority lineage and a single unique stock case and
attempt. It is neither a refund nor a replacement for the original
stock/chamber slot namespace. Preserve the spent original authority and both
original slot records, including any spent state, without overwriting, moving,
reopening or reinterpreting them.
A stock grant cannot create, renew or imply a chamber grant.

Before reservation, the future consumer must authenticate and bind:

- The owner identity and exact owner decision, grant issuer, immutable lineage
  to the spent original authority and distinct new authority identity. A
  synthetic fixture or hash alone does not prove owner consent.
- Exactly one Google stock case, its unique attempt, private owned inputs and
  permitted inspected transport. Reject duplicate, spent, foreign, modified
  or conflicting case, attempt or lineage identifiers.
- Exact source tree and source pins, browser/dependency cohort, setup, isolated
  fixture, coordination receipt and finite window. Independently authenticate
  current permission and recheck it at admission and final egress. Stale or
  historical receipts cannot pass.
- The complete current qualification index, original byte handles and hashes,
  independent actual producer completions and witnesses, and exact independent
  frame, compliance, craft, architecture and reconciliation review documents
  and receipts. Their source, binding, scope, outcome and freshness must match.
  Reject missing, duplicate, malformed, changed, unreadable, mismatched,
  synthetic or unproven provenance rather than inferring a pass.
- Exact finite start and expiry checked against trusted current time. The
  future lease is single-use, nonserializable and non-replayable. Claim,
  durable exclusive reservation and final egress fail closed on expiry,
  cancellation, loss, reentry, partial state, conflict or uncertainty. Reserve
  the distinct case durably before official source execution. Once reserved,
  failure, crash, incomplete output or a new path cannot refund or replay it.
  An intermediate check or restarted process cannot renew the lease.

The later implementation must define and independently review exact private
receipt and custody mechanics. This record contains no private authority values,
locator or inspection of the original authority.

### Unchanged inspected-reference envelope

Preserve native initialization, configuration and consent. Use the existing
owned Secondary action and a fresh transaction input. Admit at most one explicit
USD 1 conversion, never a real purchase, ad click, PII or enhanced conversion.
No unrelated tag, account, billing or campaign change follows.

The case has one absolute 60-second envelope, including settlement, cleanup and
output. It cannot reset or extend. Maximums are 20 physical vendor HTTP attempts
including retries and redirect hops, 64 observations, 2 KiB per URL, 64 KiB
request headers and body, 2 MiB accepted response and 8 MiB total retention.
Count physical attempts before forwarding. Stop before forwarding when a bound
is exhausted. An incomplete result remains incomplete.

Keep the closed Google collector policy. Only its fixed HTTPS authorities and
eligible methods, resource types, paths and queries are eligible. Bind
destination, label, document, value, currency and transaction to private owned
inputs. Response-linked and redirected destinations require their actual
completed parent response and the exact approved relationship. The
selector-free companion requires a same-case owned primary, its exact nonempty
AUID and the explicit companion flag. That correlation is not independent
ownership authentication. Unknown branches stop the case.

Recheck current permission, consent, destination, bound case inputs and deadline
immediately before each physical forwarding attempt, including retries and
redirects. Recheck after asynchronous peer authentication or upstream setup.
Cancellation cannot undo a request already issued.

Use the case-owned authenticated loopback broker, a fresh disposable browser
profile and only the case's ephemeral inspector certificate. Verify upstream
hostnames and certificates. No generic proxy, custom public upstream, wildcard,
system trust change, personal profile, TLS-key logging or retained inspector
private key is allowed. Preserve original application bytes, ordered duplicate
headers, actual response bodies and status, relationships and observed browser
cookie/storage effects. Missing or truncated required behavior cannot pass.

### Stop and retained guard

ADR-0047 and ADR-0048 continue to govern native safety. Record
`descendant_cleanup: unknown_manual` and
`complete_descendant_cleanup: false`. Complete helper exit is not a new
decision or prerequisite. Independently qualify synchronous no-forwarding
admission closure, cancellation of existing upstream sockets and settlement
of tracked authentication, relay and response work. Queued CONNECT and
existing H1/H2 tunnels cannot regain forwarding after stop or expiry.

Qualify exclusive acquisition and retention of the exact case-owned reject
endpoint without a release/rebind gap. Collision, foreign ownership or an
unexpected descriptor refuses admission. Cover controller, producer and service
loss, acquisition failure, queued connections, repeated failure, restart,
expiry, same-endpoint replacement and explicit job removal with independent
receiver evidence. Crash/restart defaults to rejection, never replay.
Establish supported logout/domain-loss safety without logging out or removing
the shared user/GUI domain. If that property is unproved, keep native admission
closed. A service-crash test or assumed helper exit cannot substitute.

After stop or loss the retained endpoint may persist solely as a local reject
guard. It cannot forward, rearm the lease, reset counters, add an event or
extend the capture. Retain it until explicit manual-cleanup confirmation and
release only verified case-owned resources using private identity-bound
recovery evidence. Close admission before browser/context shutdown, and keep
the guard until the owned root exit is observed. Preserve private original
evidence and independent producer completion on failure paths. Report root exit
and profile disposition separately. Unknown root ownership, output failure or
unqualified guard release remains blocked/incomplete. Do not scan unrelated
processes, kill by name, touch an everyday browser or delete an unverified
profile. No new runner, launcher, cleanup or custody framework is selected.

### Proposed later source-fit and qualification work

These are proposed future edits, not implementation or a pass:

1. Narrowly revise `rig/inspected-google-admission.mjs` for a distinct,
   authenticated additional-Google lineage and single-use consumer. Preserve
   original slot behavior, reject conflicting/replayed bindings and leave
   native dispatch closed until every listed prerequisite is proved.
2. Add only the needed Google-specific authority reservation and native
   lease/collector connection in the existing capture and inspected-transport
   path. Keep Meta policy and permissions unchanged. Do not generalize a proxy.
3. Reconcile `rig/inspected-google-cohort.mjs` with the actually admitted,
   independently pinned current executable/dependency cohort. Requalify all
   84 exact H1/H2 controls on the final source and cohort, including positive,
   enforcement and matched-bypass receiver observations, retained original
   bytes and independent completion/witness bindings. Seven local cases are not
   a substitute.
4. Qualify the actual native lease, closed policy and collector together,
   including physical retry/count and the 20/21 boundary, ownership, response
   parent, companion, byte/deadline limits, stop and retained-guard failure/loss
   cases. Require authenticated permission, setup, coordination, guard, current
   source and actual independent review receipts. A synthetic witness does not
   authorize native execution.

Do not turn this into a general launcher, proxy, cleanup or custody project.
The eventual source packet requires its own bounded approval and reviews.

3. Only the parent may separately admit exact finite control and capture work

The inspected reference has different TLS from the ordinary browser. Original

[059-01](../specs/059-google-ads-full-compatibility/slice-01-owned-conversion.md#L17-L83).
Keep 059-15 IN_PROGRESS, 059-01 READY_FOR_REVIEW and inherited full 059
needs-changes/non-clearing.

## Consequences

The owner can review a concrete prospective lineage without disturbing spent
authority. Implementation, current-source qualification and a separate grant
remain substantial work. The native stop and reject-guard properties remain
unproved; accepting the contract would not prove them.

## Assumptions

- Unverified: a separately reviewed consumer can authenticate the future
  lineage, permission and evidence and enforce the single-use reservation
  without modifying or reopening the original records.
- Unverified: current-cohort controls can qualify the actual Google native
  lease, collector and retained guard, including logout/domain-loss behavior.
  Historical source and local results do not supply that proof.

## Kill criteria

Keep native admission closed and revisit this proposal if the lineage cannot
remain distinct and non-replayable, or if the existing physical limits,
no-forwarding stop and retained-guard safety cannot be established.

## Open questions

The exact authenticated private receipt and reservation representation belongs
to the separately approved implementation design. If owner acceptance is
withheld or required qualification cannot be demonstrated, keep the reference
blocked.
